THE LEARNING METHOD

Don't just read.
Prove you understand.

Security knowledge becomes professional capability when you can explain a system, reproduce a failure, verify a fix and communicate the result.

THE STANDARDCan you explain it, test it, and show the evidence?
01

Understand the system

Map components, data flows, actors, assumptions and trust boundaries.

↘
02

Investigate behaviour

Read requests, code, configurations and protocol mechanics.

↘
03

Test the boundary

Change inputs, roles, states and execution order in a safe environment.

↘
04

Verify the property

Use reproducible tests, evidence and negative cases to verify controls.

↘
05

Deliver the finding

Explain impact, root cause, remediation and retest results.
